Careers

We grow by need, not by headcount targets. These are the roles we expect to open as the product, research, and client work outgrow a small team. There are no live openings today, but we keep thoughtful applications on file and reach out when the timing and scope match.

Research

Research Analyst

Closed

Study how our signals behave across market regimes, then turn that work into research notes that are rigorous enough for internal use and clear enough for the Academy audience.

What you'd do
  • Backtest and stress-test the signal across assets and regimes
  • Quantify hit rate, drawdown, and the cases where it misfires
  • Write research-grade Academy articles for a retail audience
  • Track market conditions and flag notable setups
What we'd look for
  • Background in finance, economics, or statistics
  • Comfortable with Python or R and market data
  • Writes clearly for non-experts
  • Intellectually honest about what the data does and doesn't show
Engineering

Data Scientist

Closed

Own the data and signal engine that powers the daily alerts, from ingestion and validation through backtesting, scheduling, monitoring, and deployment hygiene.

What you'd do
  • Maintain and extend the DoubleTrends™ signal pipeline end to end
  • Build and validate new features and models on market time-series
  • Safeguard data quality and reproducibility
  • Keep the daily job reliable and observable
What we'd look for
  • Strong Python (pandas, numpy)
  • Time-series analysis and a working grasp of ML
  • Experience with market-data APIs and scheduled jobs
  • Values reproducibility and tests over cleverness
Growth

Sales

Closed

Grow the subscriber base among passive, long-horizon retail investors without turning the product into hype. The role would sit at the intersection of content, partnerships, feedback, and trust.

What you'd do
  • Build top-of-funnel through content, community, and partnerships
  • Convert Academy readers into subscribers
  • Gather user feedback and relay it back to the product
  • Represent the product without overpromising
What we'd look for
  • Comfort talking to a retail-investor audience
  • Clear, trustworthy communication
  • Experience in fintech, creator, or community growth
  • Puts compliance and honesty before conversion
