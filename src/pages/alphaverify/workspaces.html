Workspaces

A workspace fixes everything a study depends on before any result is seen: the market, its data sources, the grid of moves and horizons, and the conditions to test. Three ship with AlphaVerify.

Every condition is split into ten bins by its own quantiles. Data snapshots and a cleaning version are recorded with each run, so results can be traced to the exact inputs that produced them.

NASDAQ daily: the reference workspace

The workspace behind the published findings. Prices are unadjusted daily bars for the NASDAQ Composite from Yahoo Finance; the VIX, the 10-year Treasury yield and the U.S. dollar index are taken on the same dates.

Bitcoin daily: on-chain and halving-cycle conditions

Daily bitcoin prices from Yahoo Finance, with network data from the Coin Metrics community API. Besides the standard technical families, this workspace adds conditions that exist only for bitcoin.

Technical
RSI (6), Williams %R (4), rate of change (5), stochastic (3), MACD (3), moving averages (10), volatility (9), volume (3), drawdown (7)
On-chain
Market value to realized value (MVRV), hash-rate trend, active-address trend (5)
Halving cycle
Days since the last halving, position within the cycle, days to the next (3)
Outside data
Dollar index (7)

Building a workspace

A workspace is a folder with a declared feature universe (universe.json) and a data loader (data.py); market-specific conditions can be added as a plugin, as the bitcoin workspace does for on-chain and halving data. Copy a shipped workspace with alphaverify init and edit it, or see the repository for the format.
