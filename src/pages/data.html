About back-tested results

Readings and signals dated before {{IND_LIVE_SINCE}} are hypothetical. They were produced after the fact by applying the current method to historical data, with the benefit of hindsight in its design, and were not available to anyone at the time. Back-tested results have inherent limitations and do not represent actual investment results.

Returns shown are S&P 500 index price returns. They exclude dividends, transaction costs and taxes, and the index cannot be invested in directly. Past and back-tested results do not guarantee future results.
