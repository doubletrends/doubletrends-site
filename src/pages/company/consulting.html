Capabilities

Areas of work

Data collection and pipelines

Automated collection from public web sources, APIs and documents, including JavaScript-rendered pages and PDF reports. What you get is a validated dataset (schema documented, typed, de-duplicated) as CSV, JSON or written straight to a database. Ongoing collection includes scheduling, error logging and alerting. Work is limited to sources whose terms permit automated access.

Statistical analysis and forecasting

Exploratory analysis, hypothesis testing, regression, and time-series decomposition and forecasting in R or Python — including causal designs such as difference-in-differences and regression discontinuity, and the evaluation of experiments. Each result comes with its assumptions and a plain statement of what it can and can’t conclude.

Predictive models

Classification, regression, forecasting and anomaly detection on structured data: churn, demand, risk scoring, fraud and segmentation. Models are evaluated on held-out data, and handed over with reproducible code plus a model card that covers performance by segment and the known ways it fails.

Case study · The firm’s own work

Building a point-in-time market regime classifier

The DoubleTrends Regime Indicator is the firm’s own system and the best example we have of how an engagement gets built: the question, the data, the validation and the operations are all documented in public.

The question
Classify S&P 500 market stress every trading day from public data alone, with a back-tested history that avoids hindsight wherever the data allows, and a live system that reliably publishes after every close.
Data
Daily index prices, the VIX and three Federal Reserve series. Macroeconomic downloads are validated before use and retried on failure; if the source stays unavailable, the last validated copy (at most a week old) is used and the run is flagged.
Method
Measures of the depth, duration and sharpness of a decline, dual-horizon momentum and volatility percentiles feed a four-state classification and a signal state machine. The structure of every rule is published.
Validation
Thirty years of history are computed twice, separately — over the full series and date by date with only the data each date allowed. Both must agree before anything is published. There is a known limitation in the timing of macroeconomic inputs, and it’s disclosed, not buried.
Operations
Computation scheduled to the NYSE calendar, including early closes; retried dispatch and a backup end-of-day run; and a monitoring view that flags missing or late readings.
Outputs
A methodology document, a complete signal record with a CSV download, and a daily reading delivered to subscribers.
Case study · The firm’s own work

An open-source validation framework, specified in full

AlphaVerify tests whether a trading signal’s edge survives comparison with markets that contain no signal — which is the kind of question a research engagement is there to answer straight.

The question
Does a market condition change the odds of a move by more than chance would? Answered for every range of every condition, across a grid of move sizes and horizons.
Method
Conditional move probabilities, baseline subtraction, and a null built from 1,000 synthetic markets with no memory. The full numerical specification is published.
Engineering
A staged command-line pipeline in Python and PyTorch with GPU support, written artifacts at every stage, an architecture covered by tests, and reproducible workspaces for the NASDAQ and bitcoin.
Distribution
Released on the Python Package Index under a public-domain (CC0) license, with its limitations stated right next to its findings.

How an engagement runs

  1. Scope

    Agree on the question, the data available, constraints, timeline and what a useful answer would look like.

  2. Build

    Prepare the data, run the analysis or model, document assumptions, and review interim findings with you before delivery.

  3. Deliver

    Code, datasets, charts and methodology notes, plus notes on how to maintain or extend the work.
