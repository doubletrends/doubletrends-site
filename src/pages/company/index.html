About DoubleTrends

DoubleTrends L.L.C. is an independent quantitative research firm. It studies how equity markets behave under stress, publishes its research and the DoubleTrends Regime Indicator openly, and offers research and data engineering services.

The firm

At a glance

The firm’s work centers on one question — how to tell market stress from noise — and it publishes the methods and records behind its answers.

How we are paid

Sources of income, and what we do not accept

Indicator subscriptions
Subscribers pay USD 149 a year for the daily reading and same-day signals. The fee is set to cover the servers and data services that compute and deliver the reading. The indicator’s rules, record and corrections remain public.
Research services
Engagements are scoped and billed individually.
Not accepted
DoubleTrends accepts no payments from brokers or exchanges, carries no advertising, and does not sell trading signals.
