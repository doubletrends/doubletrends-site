About the firm

DoubleTrends L.L.C. is a quantitative research firm. It studies how equity markets behave under stress, publishes its research and the DoubleTrends™ Regime Indicator in the open and offers research and data engineering services.

The firm

At a glance

The firm’s work keeps coming back to one question, how to tell market stress apart from noise, and it publishes the methods and records behind its answers.
