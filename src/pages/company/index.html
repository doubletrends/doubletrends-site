About DoubleTrends

DoubleTrends L.L.C. publishes the DoubleTrends Regime Indicator — a rules-based daily classification of U.S. equity market stress — and research on how the market behaves under stress.

The firm

At a glance

The firm does one thing: it measures whether the S&P 500 is under stress, publishes the rules it uses to decide, and keeps the complete record in public.
