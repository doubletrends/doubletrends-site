Careers

DoubleTrends hires rarely and deliberately. The roles below describe the functions the firm expects to add; applications are reviewed as each role opens, and speculative applications are welcome.

Location Toronto (remote)Applications careers@doubletrends.com

Research

Quantitative Analyst

Studies how the indicator behaves across regimes and periods: where it is robust, where it is conditional, and where it fails. Measures hit rates, the shape of forward returns and false positives across the full history, and turns findings into internal memos and research notes that stay faithful to the evidence.

A degree in statistics, mathematics, finance, physics, computer science or a similar field; three or more years of quantitative or market research; reproducible work in Python or R; writing that claims no more certainty than the evidence allows.

Research

Markets Analyst

Follows the macroeconomic and market context around the indicator — volatility, policy, labor and inflation — documents each signal and its outcome as it matures, and writes research notes that are candid about limits and useful to long-term investors.

A degree in economics, finance, statistics or a similar field; two or more years following macro or equity markets where accuracy mattered more than volume; fluency with price series, economic releases and central-bank communication.

Engineering

Data Engineer

Owns the system that makes the research run every day: ingestion, validation, persistence, internal interfaces and delivery, across a Python research pipeline and a Cloudflare Workers service. Keeps it observable, tested and reproducible, including the failure cases that only appear in production.

Strong Python, TypeScript and SQL; schema and interface design; time-series data; documentation and code review as part of the work.

How to apply. Email careers@doubletrends.com with the role, your CV or LinkedIn profile, and a short note on fit. For employment verification, include the candidate’s name, the requesting organization and your deadline.
