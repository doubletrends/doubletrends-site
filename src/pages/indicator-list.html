Volatility & Volume Analysis Series

For institutional market makers — day trading and order flow

Volume Weighted Average Price (VWAP)

The VWAP is often regarded as the market maker's secret weapon — a powerful tool leveraged by institutional traders to outmaneuver retail participants. By calculating the average price of an asset weighted by trading volume, VWAP reveals where the "smart money" is positioning itself throughout the trading session.

Institutions and high-frequency traders use VWAP to gauge fair value, manipulate market sentiment, and execute large orders without alarming the market. By incorporating VWAP into your strategy, you gain insight into the hidden dynamics of price action.

Session Volume Profile

The Session Volume Profile provides access to the market's most crucial information — volume action — something that retail traders are often blind to. While traditional price action analysis relies solely on drawing lines on a price chart, it misses the most important signal: where the real trading volume is happening.

Institutions and market makers know that volume, not just price, dictates market movement. The Session Volume Profile reveals how volume is distributed across price levels, showing where the market is truly in play. It identifies high-volume nodes (HVNs) and low-volume nodes (LVNs) — critical levels where price is likely to reverse or consolidate.
