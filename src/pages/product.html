What exactly do I receive? ⌄

A same-day alert in your portal on signal day, plus the complete live picture. The Market Brief shows today's regime classification, VIX, Fed Funds, CPI, and unemployment with live alert and signal status. The Signal Terminal charts 20 years of S&P 500 price history with every oversold alert and signal marked on the chart, regime-colored bar by bar — updated every trading day.

How often will it fire? ⌄

Rarely. Since 2016, the S&P 500 index version has produced {{TR_SIGNAL_COUNT}} signals. Some years have several; some have none. That is the point.

Is this financial advice? ⌄

No. DoubleTrends™ is educational, rules-based market information only. It is not a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any security.

What does DoubleTrends™ measure? ⌄

A regime filter plus a dual Williams %R reversal rule on the daily S&P 500. It only fires in panic or bear regimes, with bear-market reversals filtered harder.

Which assets are covered? ⌄

The S&P 500 index today. ETF investors can express it with VOO, SPY, or IVV. Nasdaq 100 support, including QQQ, is planned.

Does a signal mean I should buy immediately? ⌄

No. A signal is a timing reference, not a personal recommendation. Some investors use it to review a planned contribution or staged entry; others do nothing.
