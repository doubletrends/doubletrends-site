Questions

Common questions

How often does it fire?
Rarely: {{SIG_COUNT}} times since {{SIG_FIRST}}, about {{SIG_PER_DECADE}} per decade. Some years have several signals and many have none.
Is a signal a recommendation to buy?
No. It describes market conditions. Some investors use it to review a planned contribution or a staged entry; others take no action. It is not advice and does not consider your circumstances.
Which markets are covered?
The S&P 500 index. Investors commonly hold it through funds such as VOO, SPY or IVV. Coverage of the Nasdaq-100 is planned.
How was the history produced?
Readings before {{IND_LIVE_SINCE}} are a back-tested reconstruction. The methodology explains how, and the historical data page lists every signal.
