The DoubleTrends Regime Indicator

A rules-based classification of U.S. equity market stress. Each trading day, the S&P 500 is assigned one of four regimes — calm, correction, panic or bear. A reversal signal is eligible to fire only under stress. DoubleTrends publishes the indicator, its methodology and its complete record as research.

Why the daily reading has a fee

DoubleTrends does not sell trading signals. The indicator is published as research: its rules and its complete record are public on this site.

Computing and delivering the reading each trading day runs on servers and data services the firm rents. Subscribers receive each day’s reading shortly after the close and any signal the same day, for USD 149 a year — a fee set to cover that upkeep.
