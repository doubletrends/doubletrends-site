Open-source research

AlphaVerify: does a signal beat chance?

An open-source framework that tests whether a trading signal’s apparent edge beats 1,000 synthetic markets containing no signal. Popular conditions often do not: on the NASDAQ since 2015, the golden cross shows a 24.5-point swing in the odds — and signal-free markets produce one at least as strong 8.4% of the time.
