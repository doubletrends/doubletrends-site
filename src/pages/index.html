Open-source research

AlphaVerify: does a signal beat chance?

An open-source framework that pits a trading signal’s apparent edge against 1,000 synthetic markets with no signal in them. Popular conditions often lose: on the NASDAQ since 2015, the golden cross shows a 24.5-point swing in the odds — yet signal-free markets turn up one at least as strong 8.4% of the time.
